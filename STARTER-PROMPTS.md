# Claude + ValuEdge — starter prompts (submission and onboarding)

Use these sample prompts in the **plugin listing**, reviewer instructions, help documentation and appropriate onboarding materials. Display as examples, not as claims that the response will always contain complete market data.

## Public or introductory stock discovery

1. "Find the correct ticker for Apple and show what ValuEdge can research about it."
2. "Use ValuEdge to screen undervalued US stocks with a margin of safety and explain the filters used."
3. "Compare MSFT and GOOGL fundamentals and available fair values, including source dates."
4. "Explain how ValuEdge's reported intrinsic value differs from the market price for AAPL."

## Daily data and dividend research

5. "Check NVDA's latest **daily** RSI, SMA 20/50/200 and trend, noting the data-as-of date."
6. "Show the reported dividend calendar for KO, distinguishing confirmed future dates from historical payments."

## Connected-account workflows (OAuth required)

7. "Analyze my ValuEdge portfolio concentration, diversification and available valuation coverage."
8. "Review my ValuEdge Watchlist, identify holdings lacking a reliable fair value, and explain what is unavailable."
9. "Show my ValuEdge Smart Alerts and explain their latest reported changes."
10. "Summarize the available dividend data in my ValuEdge tracker."

## Explicit actions only (requires prior confirmation)

11. "Add MSFT to my ValuEdge Watchlist after confirming the correct list and ticker."
12. "Review a portfolio transaction record and help me correct it after showing me the exact proposed edit."

**Do not advertise:** executing brokerage trades; real-time market data where only daily historical data is available; guaranteed valuation accuracy; nonexistent backtesting MCP tools; cross-account access; all features without plan or OAuth requirements.

**Discovery note:** No public evidence guarantees that listing these prompts directly changes ranking. Their first purpose is to help prospective users reach a real, useful result.
